Final Project Proposal

Parkour Playlist

  • Side-scrolling parkour where you discover different songs by moving through the level
  • Buttons/checkpoints play new tracks, shift colors, and show song cards when you land on them
  • The level becomes an interactive playlist you explore by running and jumping
  • Simple mechanics (run, jump, collide) plus interactive audio, color and animation tied to each song, clean responsive look
  • Goal: make finding music feel playful and physical, with movement as the interface
